PodSherpa plugins (Southern Digital) - test build

These are early test builds: plain interface, first-draft sound. Three plugins:
PodSherpa Basecamp (the master rack), PodSherpa Summit, PodSherpa Dropoff.

macOS
  1. Copy everything in VST3 to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  2. Copy everything in AU to     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  3. These builds are not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks them
     until you clear the download flag. Open Terminal and run:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/PodSherpa*.vst3
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/PodSherpa*.component
  4. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Windows
  1. Copy everything in VST3 to   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  2. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
