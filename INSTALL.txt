PodSherpa plugins (Southern Digital FX) - test build

Early test builds: plain first-pass interface, the suite look is not in yet.
Twelve plugins - eleven repair and voice modules, plus the rack that holds them.

  PodSherpa Basecamp          the master rack - load any of the modules below into its slots
  PodSherpa Dropoff           high-pass filter, six slopes
  PodSherpa Scree             click remover
  PodSherpa Gust              de-plosive (the thump on a P or B)
  PodSherpa Clearing          noise reduction, learns the room
  PodSherpa Serac             de-esser, can learn where the sibilance sits
  PodSherpa Contour           five-band voice EQ
  PodSherpa Contour Dynamic   five-band dynamic EQ
  PodSherpa Traverse          speech compressor
  PodSherpa Ridgeline         four-band compressor
  PodSherpa Altitude          voice leveler
  PodSherpa Summit            look-ahead true-peak limiter, reads integrated loudness

macOS
  1. Copy everything in VST3 to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  2. Copy everything in AU to     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  3. These builds are not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks them
     until you clear the download flag. Open Terminal and run:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/PodSherpa*.vst3
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/PodSherpa*.component
  4. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Windows
  1. Copy everything in VST3 to   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  2. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

What to check on this build
  - Every one of the twelve loads without hanging the DAW.
  - Controls move audio while playback is running, not just when stopped.
  - Dropoff's Phase switch flipped mid-playback does not click or drop out.
  - Serac on a sibilant guest: Learn, then listen for lisping as Sensitivity goes past 50.
  - Gust's Listen on a thumpy recording: you should hear thumps, not the bottom of the voice.
  - Summit's loudness readout against whatever you normally trust for LUFS.
  - Save a project, reopen it, and check every control came back where you left it.
